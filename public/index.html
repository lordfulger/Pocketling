        Pet book

        Every kind of pet you've raised. Each animal grows into one of four adults depending on how well it's cared for. The starry ones are only for perfect parents.

        How to take care of it

          How it grows

          Egg→Baby→Child→Teen→Adult

          The egg hatches after a minute. The baby needs a lot of attention for its first hour, then grows into a child. It becomes a teen after one day and an adult after three. Care mistakes and manners decide which teen and adult it becomes.

          Children go to bed around 8 pm and older pets between 9 and 11 pm. They wake up around 8 to 10 am. A well-kept adult lives for two to three weeks.

          Friends, marriage and babies

          Create a home and send the invite link to someone special. When you both have Pocketling open, your pets visit each other, play and grow closer. Two adult pets with full friendship hearts can marry. Married pets can start a family: each of you gets the baby's egg, and you can raise it when your current pet passes on.

          To keep it one tap away, add this page to your home screen: in Safari use Share, then Add to Home Screen; in Chrome use the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
